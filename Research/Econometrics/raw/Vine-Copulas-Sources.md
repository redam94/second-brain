# Primary sources for vine copula notes

## Aas, Czado, Frigessi & Bakken (2009)
**Title:** "Pair-copula constructions of multiple dependence"  
**Journal:** Insurance: Mathematics and Economics, 44(2), pp. 182–198  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.insmatheco.2007.02.001  
**Free version:** LMU Munich SFB 386 Working Paper 487 (2006)  
URL: https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/1855/1/paper_487.pdf *(policy-blocked in session)*  
**Content:** Introduces pair-copula constructions (PCC) in an inferential setting; develops the simplifying assumption; derives the C-vine and D-vine likelihood; sequential ML and IFM estimation.

## Bedford & Cooke (2001)
**Title:** "Probability density decomposition for conditionally dependent random variables modeled by vines"  
**Journal:** Annals of Combinatorics, 5(1), pp. 245–268  
**Content:** First rigorous graph-theoretic treatment of vine structures; shows any multivariate distribution can be decomposed into bivariate copulas via a vine.

## Bedford & Cooke (2002)
**Title:** "Vines — a new graphical model for dependent random variables"  
**Journal:** Annals of Statistics, 30(4), pp. 1031–1068  
**DOI:** 10.1214/aos/1031689016  
**Open access:** Project Euclid *(policy-blocked in session)*  
URL: https://projecteuclid.org/journals/annals-of-statistics/volume-30/issue-4/Vines--a-new-graphical-model-for-dependent-random-variables/10.1214/aos/1031689016.full  
**Content:** Canonical paper defining regular vines (R-vines), C-vines, D-vines and the proximity condition; proves that C-vines and D-vines are the most tractable special cases.

## Czado (2010)
**Title:** "Pair-copula constructions of multivariate copulas" (lecture chapter)  
**In:** Jaworski et al. (eds.), Copula Theory and Its Applications, Springer Lecture Notes in Statistics  
**Free version:** TUM MediaTUM repository  
URL: https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1079253/file.pdf *(policy-blocked in session)*  
**Content:** Pedagogical overview of PCC, vine structures, and their application; covers model selection and inference.

## Czado & Nagler (2022)
**Title:** "Vine copula based modeling"  
**Journal:** Annual Review of Statistics and Its Application, 9, pp. 453–477  
**DOI:** 10.1146/annurev-statistics-040220-101153  
**Content:** Modern survey; covers R-vines, truncated vines, non-simplified vines, Bayesian estimation.

## Brechmann & Schepsmeier (2013)
**Title:** "Modeling dependence with C- and D-vine copulas: The R package CDVine"  
**Journal:** Journal of Statistical Software, 52(3), pp. 1–27  
**Content:** Software reference for the CDVine R package; also a good applied introduction. Superseded by VineCopula package (Schepsmeier et al.).
