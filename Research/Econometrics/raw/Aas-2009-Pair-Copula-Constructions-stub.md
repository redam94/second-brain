## Abstract Summary

Proposes pair-copula constructions (PCCs) for modelling multivariate data with
tail dependence. Each variable is linked to others through a cascade of bivariate
copulas ("pair copulas"), applied to original variables and conditional pseudo-
observations. Covers C-vines and D-vines as two canonical tree structures, derives
the density factorization, and presents an ML estimation procedure based on
Joe (1996). Applies to financial returns and Norwegian weather data.

## Key Contributions

1. Formal definition of C-vine and D-vine density factorizations
2. ML estimation via sequential pseudo-observations (conditional CDFs)
3. Demonstration that standard bivariate copula families (Gaussian, t, Clayton, Gumbel)
   can serve as pair copula "building blocks"
4. Shows flexibility: different pair copulas at different tree levels
