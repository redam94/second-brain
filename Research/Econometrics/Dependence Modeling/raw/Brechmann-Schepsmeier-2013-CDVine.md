# Brechmann & Schepsmeier (2013)

**Modeling Dependence with C- and D-Vine Copulas: The R Package CDVine**
*Journal of Statistical Software* 52(3): 1–27.

Presents the CDVine R package for C- and D-vine copula inference. Contains detailed
descriptions of the h-function implementations, sequential estimation algorithm, model
selection, goodness-of-fit tests, and worked examples. CDVine is now superseded by the
VineCopula package (Schepsmeier et al.) which extends to general R-vines.
