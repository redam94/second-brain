Title:     Pair-copula constructions of multiple dependence
Authors:   Kjersti Aas, Claudia Czado, Arnoldo Frigessi, Henrik Bakken
Published: Insurance: Mathematics and Economics, 44(2), 182–198, 2009
DOI:       https://doi.org/10.1016/j.insmatheco.2007.02.001

Free version (working paper, 2006):
  LMU Munich Sonderforschungsbereich 386, Paper 487
  URL: https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/1855/1/paper_487.pdf
  (45 pages; all four authors; content substantially matches the 2009 journal article)

Abstract (from journal):
  The article constructs pair-copula models for multivariate data using the pair copula
  decomposition of a general multivariate distribution. It introduces regular vines
  (R-vines) as the graphical framework for organising the bivariate building blocks and
  derives closed-form expressions for the density of two important special cases: the
  C-vine (canonical vine) and the D-vine (drawable vine). It develops maximum-likelihood
  and sequential estimation methods for these models, and illustrates them on Norwegian
  financial data, where the pair-copula approach substantially outperforms the Gaussian
  and t-copula benchmarks.

Key contributions:
  1. Operationalises the Bedford & Cooke (2002) vine framework for statistical estimation.
  2. Provides tractable density formulas for C-vine and D-vine pair copula constructions.
  3. Derives the h-function (conditional CDF transform) used in sequential sampling.
  4. Proposes sequential and full ML estimation procedures.
  5. Demonstrates superior fit to multivariate financial data via AIC/BIC model selection.
