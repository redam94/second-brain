## Abstract (from memory / known content)

Builds on Bedford & Cooke (2001, 2002) and Joe (1996) to develop **pair-copula constructions (PCC)** of multivariate distributions: decomposes the joint density into d univariate marginal densities and d(d-1)/2 bivariate conditional copula densities. Introduces the **D-vine** and **C-vine** as two special tractable regular vine structures and provides sequential maximum likelihood estimation. Applied to two empirical datasets.

## Key sections

- Sec. 1: Introduction — curse of dimensionality for multivariate copulas; bivariate copula building blocks
- Sec. 2: Pair-copula decompositions — density decomposition, C-vine and D-vine constructions, h-functions
- Sec. 3: Estimation — sequential MLE for C- and D-vines
- Sec. 4: Simulation — algorithms for C- and D-vine sampling
- Sec. 5: Applications — Norwegian financial data (4-dim) and Norwegian exchange rates (5-dim)
- Appendix: Proof of density decomposition uniqueness
