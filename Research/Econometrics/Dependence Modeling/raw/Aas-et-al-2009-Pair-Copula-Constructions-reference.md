## Abstract (reconstructed from training knowledge)

Statistical inference for multivariate distributions using copulas requires flexible, tractable
models for the dependence structure. For high dimensions, existing parametric copulas are either
too restrictive (Gaussian: symmetric, zero tail dependence) or not identifiable. This paper
develops the pair-copula construction (PCC) based on Bedford and Cooke's (2001, 2002) vine
graphical model. Any joint density factorises into marginals and a product of bivariate
(conditional) copula densities arranged in a d-dimensional vine. Two special cases are
presented: D-vine (path-shaped trees) and C-vine (star-shaped trees). The paper provides a
sequential maximum likelihood estimator and applies the method to Norwegian financial data,
demonstrating that vine copulas with tail-dependent pair copulas outperform the Gaussian copula.

## Key Contributions

1. Provides statistical estimation framework for Bedford-Cooke vine models
2. Derives recursive formulae for conditional CDFs via "h-functions"
3. Proposes sequential MLE: estimate pair copulas sequentially from Tree 1 to Tree d-1
4. Demonstrates flexible tail dependence: each pair copula can have its own family
5. Application: 4-dimensional Norwegian financial returns (oil price, USD/NOK, equities, bonds)
