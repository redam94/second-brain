# Aas, Czado, Frigessi & Bakken (2009)

**Pair-copula constructions of multiple dependence**
*Insurance: Mathematics and Economics* 44(2): 182–198.

The foundational reference for pair-copula constructions (PCCs) in statistics/econometrics.
Introduces C-vine and D-vine as special cases of Bedford & Cooke's regular vine theory, with
sequential MLE estimation via h-functions and an application to financial return data.
