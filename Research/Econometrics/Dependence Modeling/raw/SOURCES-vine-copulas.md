# Vine Copula Source Documents

Notes in this batch were written from established mathematical knowledge of the following freely-available papers. Direct PDF download was not possible in this execution environment (network proxy blocks external HTTPS for academic repositories), but the papers are freely available at the URLs listed below.

## Primary Sources

### 1. Aas, Czado, Frigessi & Bakken (2009)
**Title:** "Pair-copula constructions of multiple dependence"
**Journal:** Insurance: Mathematics and Economics, 44(2), pp. 182–198
**Free URL:** https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/1855/1/paper_487.pdf (LMU Munich preprint)
**Also at:** https://mistis.inrialpes.fr/docs/pair-copula-construction.pdf (INRIA)
**Content:** The foundational practical paper introducing sequential MLE for vine copulas (C-vines and D-vines), h-functions for computing conditional distributions, and worked financial examples. Bedford & Cooke's theory made operationally useful.

### 2. Bedford & Cooke (2002)
**Title:** "Vines — a new graphical model for dependent random variables"
**Journal:** Annals of Statistics, 30(4), pp. 1031–1068
**Free URL:** https://projecteuclid.org/euclid.aos/1031689016 (Project Euclid — open access for Ann. Stat.)
**Content:** The foundational paper introducing regular vines (R-vines), the proximity condition, C-vines and D-vines as special cases, and the density factorization theorem.

### 3. Czado & Nagler (2022)
**Title:** "Vine Copula Based Modeling"
**Journal:** Annual Review of Statistics and Its Application, 9, pp. 453–477
**Free URL (preprint):** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4065371 (SSRN)
**DOI:** 10.1146/annurev-statistics-040220-101153
**Content:** Comprehensive modern review: vine structure, model selection (Dissmann algorithm), inference, software (VineCopula R package), extensions, and comparison with factor copulas.

### 4. Dissmann, Brechmann, Czado & Kurowicka (2013)
**Title:** "Selecting and estimating regular vine copulae and application to financial returns"
**Journal:** Computational Statistics & Data Analysis, 59, pp. 52–69
**Free URL:** https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1079258/file.pdf (TUM preprint)
**Content:** The Dissmann et al. greedy model-selection algorithm for R-vine structure, AIC/BIC for bivariate copula family selection, application to DAX 30 daily returns.
