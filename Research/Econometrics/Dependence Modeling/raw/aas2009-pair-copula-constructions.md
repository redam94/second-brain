# Aas, Czado, Frigessi & Bakken (2009)

**Title:** Pair-copula constructions of multiple dependence  
**Journal:** Insurance: Mathematics and Economics, 44(2), 182–198  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.insmatheco.2007.02.001  

**Free working-paper version (LMU Munich SFB 386 Paper 487, 2006):**  
https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/1855/1/paper_487.pdf  
*(Host unreachable from this session — network policy blocks epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de)*

**Abstract (from search):**  
Building on the work of Joe (1996) and Bedford & Cooke (2001, 2002), constructs multivariate distributions with complex dependence structures from a cascade of bivariate pair-copulas. Introduces the C-vine and D-vine as canonical vine structures. Develops the h-function recursion for density computation and a sequential maximum likelihood estimation procedure. Applied to financial returns and Norwegian insurance data.
