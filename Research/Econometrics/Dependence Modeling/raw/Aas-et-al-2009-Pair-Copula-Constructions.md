# Aas et al. (2009) — Pair-Copula Constructions of Multiple Dependence

**Full citation:** Aas, K., Czado, C., Frigessi, A., & Bakken, H. (2009). Pair-copula constructions of multiple dependence. *Insurance: Mathematics and Economics*, 44(2), 182–198.

**Working paper version (2006):** SFB 386 Statistical Analysis and Data Modelling, Paper 487. Munich: LMU.
Free PDF (when accessible): https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/1855/1/paper_487.pdf

## Abstract

Building on the work of Joe (1996) and Bedford & Cooke (2001, 2002), the authors
develop the pair-copula construction framework for multivariate distributions.
The joint density is decomposed into a product of bivariate copula densities (pair
copulas) operating on conditional marginals. Two canonical tree structures are
studied: the C-vine (one dominant variable per tree) and D-vine (path structure).
Sequential maximum likelihood estimation is proposed. Applied to financial return
data (Norwegian and US equities plus crude oil).

## Key contributions

1. Made the Bedford-Cooke vine construction operational for statistical inference.
2. Derived explicit density formulas for C-vine and D-vine.
3. Proposed sequential (tree-by-tree) ML estimation.
4. Showed that conditional distribution functions can be computed recursively from
   pair copulas (h-function).
5. Applied to 4-dimensional financial returns with comparison to Gaussian/t copulas.
