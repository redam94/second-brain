# Czado (2010) — Pair-Copula Constructions of Multivariate Copulas

**Author:** Claudia Czado
**In:** Dependence Modeling: Vine Copula Handbook, eds. Kurowicka & Joe, World Scientific, 2011, pp. 93–109
**Preprint PDF (attempted):** https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1079253/file.pdf

## Abstract (from known content)

Survey chapter presenting the pair-copula construction (PCC) framework for multivariate copulas; covers the general density decomposition based on Bedford & Cooke (2001, 2002), the special C-vine and D-vine sub-classes, h-functions, and sequential estimation. Serves as an accessible exposition of the Aas et al. (2009) framework.
