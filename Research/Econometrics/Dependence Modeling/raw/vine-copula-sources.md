# Vine Copula Sources

These sources were identified as freely available but could not be downloaded during the 2026-10-09 ingest session due to network egress policy restrictions on arxiv.org, jstatsoft.org, and epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de.

## Primary Sources

### Aas, Czado, Frigessi & Bakken (2009)
**Title:** Pair-copula constructions of multiple dependence  
**Journal:** *Insurance: Mathematics and Economics*, 44(2), 182–198  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.insmatheco.2007.02.001  
**Free version:** Working paper preprint (2006), Sonderforschungsbereich 386, Paper 487, LMU Munich — epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/1855/1/paper_487.pdf (blocked at download time)  
**Key contribution:** Introduced the pair-copula construction (PCC) framework for C-vines and D-vines; sequential MLE via h-functions; bivariate copula family selection.

### Bedford & Cooke (2002)
**Title:** Vines — a new graphical model for dependent random variables  
**Journal:** *Annals of Statistics*, 30(4), 1031–1068  
**Key contribution:** Defined regular vines (R-vines) and proved that any n-dimensional distribution has a vine representation via a sequence of n−1 trees.

### Bedford & Cooke (2001)
**Title:** Probability density decomposition for conditionally dependent random variables modeled by vines  
**Journal:** *Annals of Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence*, 32(1–4), 245–268  
**Key contribution:** First vine construction — pair-copula decomposition of n-dimensional densities.

### Dißmann, Brechmann, Czado & Kurowicka (2013)
**Title:** Selecting and estimating regular vine copulae and application to financial returns  
**Journal:** *Computational Statistics & Data Analysis*, 59, 52–69  
**arXiv:** 1202.2002 (blocked at download time)  
**Key contribution:** Greedy maximum-spanning-tree algorithm for R-vine structure selection; application to DAX 30 stock returns.

### Brechmann & Schepsmeier (2013)
**Title:** Modeling dependence with C- and D-vine copulas: The R package CDVine  
**Journal:** *Journal of Statistical Software*, 52(3)  
**URL:** jstatsoft.org/v52/i03 (open access, blocked at download time)  
**Key contribution:** R implementation CDVine; introductory exposition of C- and D-vine estimation; superseded by VineCopula package.

### Czado (2019)
**Title:** Analyzing Dependent Data with Vine Copulas: A Practical Guide with R  
**Publisher:** Springer Lecture Notes in Statistics  
**ISBN:** 978-3-030-13784-7  
**Key contribution:** Textbook treatment; R workflows; structure selection, goodness-of-fit, truncation strategies.
