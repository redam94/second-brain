# Dissmann, Brechmann, Czado & Kurowicka (2013)

**Title:** Selecting and estimating regular vine copulae and application to financial returns  
**Journal:** Computational Statistics & Data Analysis, 59(1), 52–69  
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.2002 (submitted Apr 2012)  

**Free arXiv PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1202.2002

**Abstract (from search):**  
Develops a sequential algorithm for selecting the structure and copula families of a regular vine (R-vine). Shows how to evaluate the density of arbitrary R-vine specifications via the R-vine matrix representation. Proposes a greedy maximum-spanning-tree algorithm for tree-by-tree structure selection based on Kendall's τ, followed by AIC/BIC copula family selection. Applied to financial returns; includes C-vine and D-vine as special cases. Basis for the VineCopula R package.
