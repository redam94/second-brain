## Overview

Quantum mechanics describes systems with quantized energy states, wave-particle duality, the uncertainty principle, quantum entanglement, and probabilistic measurement outcomes (Born rule). It is the foundation for quantum field theory, quantum chemistry, and quantum computing.

Key formalism: the Schrödinger equation governs time evolution of the wave function $\psi$, which encodes probability amplitudes for measurement outcomes.
