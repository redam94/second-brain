# Quantum Field Theory

> [!summary]
> Quantum field theory (QFT) combines quantum mechanics, special relativity, and classical field theory into a framework for describing subatomic particles and their interactions. It is peripheral to this vault's focus but was ingested as a reference clipping.

> [!warning] Out of Scope
> This note covers theoretical physics. The vault focuses on Bayesian statistics, econometrics, and research methodology. This note is a minimal stub preserving the clipping for reference only.

## Overview

QFT treats particles as excitations of underlying quantum fields permeating spacetime. The Standard Model of particle physics is a QFT with gauge symmetry group $\text{SU}(3) \times \text{SU}(2) \times \text{U}(1)$. Key historical developments: quantum electrodynamics (QED), renormalization, Yang–Mills theory, the Higgs mechanism.
