# Reference: Chickering (2002)

**Title:** Optimal Structure Identification With Greedy Search

**Author:** David Maxwell Chickering

**Journal:** Journal of Machine Learning Research, Volume 3, pp. 507–554, 2002

**JMLR page:** https://jmlr.org/papers/v3/chickering02b.html

**Abstract:** The paper presents the Greedy Equivalence Search (GES) algorithm
for learning the structure of Bayesian networks from observational data.
GES operates in two phases over the space of equivalence classes (CPDAGs):
a forward phase that greedily adds edges and a backward phase that greedily
removes edges. The paper proves that GES identifies the generating model
(the optimal CPDAG by BIC score) in the large-sample limit when data are
generated by a faithful Bayesian network.

**Key contributions:**
1. Forward Equivalence Search (FES): greedy CPDAG-space edge insertion
2. Backward Equivalence Search (BES): greedy CPDAG-space edge deletion
3. Proof that GES finds the I-optimal CPDAG (Meek Conjecture proved)
4. Score decomposability as the key computational property enabling GES
5. Theorem: GES is globally optimal in the limit under faithfulness + score consistency
