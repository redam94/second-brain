# Source: Spirtes, Glymour & Scheines (2000) — Causation, Prediction, and Search

**Authors:** Peter Spirtes, Clark Glymour, Richard Scheines  
**Title:** *Causation, Prediction, and Search*, 2nd edition  
**Publisher:** MIT Press, 2000 (1st ed. 1993, Springer)  
**Free access:** Available from CMU philosophy department  
**URL:** https://www.cs.cmu.edu/afs/cs.cmu.edu/project/learn-43/lib/photoz/.g/scottd/bbbb/sgs.pdf

## What it covers

The foundational text for constraint-based causal discovery. Chapters 5–6 introduce
the **PC algorithm** (named for Peter and Clark) and the **FCI algorithm** (which
relaxes the causal sufficiency assumption to allow latent variables). The book
establishes the theoretical framework connecting conditional independence, d-separation,
the faithfulness assumption, and the PAG/CPDAG representation.

Key content relevant to the vault:
- §2: Conditional independence, d-separation, Markov condition, faithfulness assumption
- §5: The **SGS algorithm** (ancestral graph learning) and its computational intractability
- §5.4.2: The **PC algorithm** as a practical efficient variant of SGS
- §6: The **FCI algorithm** (allows latent confounders)
- §8: **IC algorithm** (Pearl 1988, effectively equivalent to PC)
