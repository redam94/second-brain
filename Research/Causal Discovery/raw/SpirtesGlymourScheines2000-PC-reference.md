# Spirtes, Glymour & Scheines (2000) — Causation, Prediction, and Search (2nd Ed.)

**Full citation:** Spirtes, P., Glymour, C., & Scheines, R. (2000). *Causation, Prediction, and Search*
(2nd ed.). MIT Press. (1st ed. 1993, Springer-Verlag.)

**Publisher:** MIT Press (freely available via CMU:
  https://www.cs.cmu.edu/afs/cs.cmu.edu/project/learn-43/lib/photoz/.g/web/.g/scottd/fullbook.pdf
  though blocked by proxy)

**Key chapters for PC algorithm:**
- Chapter 5 — The PC algorithm (skeleton + v-structure + Meek rules)
- Chapter 6 — FCI algorithm (relaxing causal sufficiency)
- Chapter 10 — Consistency proofs

**Overview of contribution:**
The book systematizes causal discovery as a formal problem. It introduces:
- The Causal Markov Condition (graph encodes conditional independencies)
- Faithfulness (all and only entailed CI relations hold in data)
- The PC algorithm (named after the first initials of the authors: Peter and Clark)
- The FCI algorithm (relaxes causal sufficiency assumption)
- Correctness proofs for both algorithms in the large-sample limit
