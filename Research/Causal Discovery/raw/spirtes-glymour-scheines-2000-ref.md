# Bibliographic Reference

**Authors:** Peter Spirtes, Clark Glymour, Richard Scheines  
**Year:** 2000  
**Publisher:** MIT Press (Adaptive Computation and Machine Learning series)  
**Edition:** 2nd (1st ed. 1993)  
**ISBN:** 978-0-262-19440-2

## Abstract / Summary

Foundational text on causal discovery from observational data. Introduces the PC algorithm (named for Peter Spirtes and Clark Glymour), the FCI algorithm (for settings with latent variables), and the theoretical framework of causal Bayes nets. Proves identifiability and consistency results for constraint-based discovery under the Markov, faithfulness, and causal sufficiency assumptions.

**Key topics:**
- Formal framework for causal inference: Markov condition, faithfulness assumption, causal sufficiency
- PC algorithm: skeleton discovery via CI tests + orientation via v-structures and Meek rules
- FCI algorithm: extension for settings with unmeasured confounders (PAGs instead of DAGs)
- Identifiability results: equivalence class (CPDAG) is the best identifiable structure under faithfulness
- Connections between interventional and observational distributions
