## Relevance to vault

This book introduces the **PC algorithm** (Ch. 5–6) — the foundational constraint-based causal discovery method. Also covers:
- FCI algorithm (for hidden variables)
- Causal Markov condition and faithfulness assumption
- Tetrad software
- Connection to interventions and policy

The PC algorithm is named after the authors **P**eter Spirtes and **C**lark Glymour.

## Key chapters

- Ch. 3: Conditional independence and Markov conditions
- Ch. 5: Discovering causal structure with the PC algorithm
- Ch. 6: Tetrad and FCI algorithms
- Ch. 7: Interventions and policy
