# Citation: Colombo & Maathuis (2014)

**Authors:** Diego Colombo, Marloes H. Maathuis
**Title:** Order-Independent Constraint-Based Causal Structure Learning
**Journal:** Journal of Machine Learning Research, Vol. 15, pp. 3921–3962, 2014
**arXiv:** 1211.3295 [stat.ME]
**JMLR URL:** https://jmlr.org/papers/v15/colombo14a.html

## Abstract (from search results)

Shows that the standard PC algorithm's output is order-dependent (different variable orderings
can yield different CPDAGs). Proposes Stable-PC and RFCI modifications that remove order-dependence
by updating adjacency sets only after all CI tests at a given conditioning level are completed.

## Key Results

- Order-dependence in standard PC: demonstrated in high-dimensional settings
- Stable-PC: collects all level-l results before updating adjacency sets
- The pcalg R package implements Stable-PC, RFCI, and related methods
- Order-independent skeleton (Stable-PC-Skeleton) and full CPDAG estimation
- Conservative-PC and conservative-FCI: majority-rule approach for v-structure orientation
