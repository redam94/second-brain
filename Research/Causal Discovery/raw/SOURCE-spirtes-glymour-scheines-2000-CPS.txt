Summary:
The foundational reference for the PC algorithm (named after Peter and Clark).
Chapters relevant to structure learning:
- Ch. 2: Graphs and Conditional Independences
- Ch. 3: Causal Theories, Markov Conditions, Faithfulness
- Ch. 5: Discovery Algorithms for Causally Sufficient Structures (PC algorithm, FCI)
- Ch. 6: Prediction

The PC algorithm (§5.4) works in two phases:
1. Skeleton discovery: Start from complete undirected graph. For increasing l=0,1,...,
   test X ⊥⊥ Y | S for all subsets S ⊆ adj(X)\{Y} of size l. Remove edge if independent.
2. Orientation: Orient v-structures (X-Z-Y where Z ∉ sep(X,Y)), then apply Meek rules.

Assumptions:
- Causal sufficiency (no latent common causes)
- Faithfulness (all independence in distribution matches d-separation in DAG)
- IID data
