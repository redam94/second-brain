Chickering, D. M. (2002). Optimal structure identification with greedy search.
Journal of Machine Learning Research, 3, 507–554.

Open-access URL: https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume3/chickering02b/chickering02b.pdf

Abstract (from JMLR listing):
The problem of finding the optimal Bayesian network for a given dataset is known to be
NP-hard. In practice, heuristic search algorithms are used. Chickering proves the
"Meek Conjecture": if a DAG H is an independence map (I-map) of a DAG G, then there
exists a sequence of edge additions and covered edge reversals transforming H into G
while preserving I-map status. This result implies that GES — a two-phase greedy search
over equivalence classes of DAGs using a decomposable score such as BIC or BDeu — is
correct and complete: it identifies a perfect map of the generative distribution when
one exists as a DAG.
