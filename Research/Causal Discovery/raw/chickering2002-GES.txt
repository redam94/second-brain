Chickering, D. M. (2002).
Optimal Structure Identification With Greedy Search.
Journal of Machine Learning Research, 3, 507–554.

Freely available at:
  https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume3/chickering02b/chickering02b.pdf
  https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v3/chickering02b.html

Key contributions:
- GES (Greedy Equivalence Search) algorithm for score-based DAG learning
- Proof of the Meek Conjecture: any DAG can be reached from any other via
  covered edge reversals, passing through intermediate CPDAGs
- Optimality theorem: GES recovers the true CPDAG under faithfulness + Gaussian noise
- Efficient operators on CPDAGs for greedy search
- Two-phase algorithm (forward insert + backward delete)
