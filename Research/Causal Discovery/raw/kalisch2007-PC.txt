Kalisch, M. & Bühlmann, P. (2007). Estimating high-dimensional directed acyclic graphs
with the PC-Algorithm.
Journal of Machine Learning Research, 8, 613–636.

Open-access URLs:
  JMLR:  https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v8/kalisch07a.html
  arXiv: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0510436

Key contribution:
Proves consistency of the PC algorithm for very high-dimensional, sparse DAGs with
Gaussian distribution, where the number of nodes d is allowed to grow with sample size n
as fast as O(n^a) for any 0 < a < ∞, under the assumption that the maximal neighbourhood
size k grows at most as O(n^b) for b < 1/2. Provides the foundational theoretical
justification for the PC algorithm in the high-dimensional setting.

Note: For the original PC algorithm, see:
Spirtes, P., Glymour, C. & Scheines, R. (2000). Causation, Prediction, and Search.
