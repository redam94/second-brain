Kalisch, M., & Bühlmann, P. (2007).
Estimating High-Dimensional Directed Acyclic Graphs with the PC-Algorithm.
Journal of Machine Learning Research, 8, 613–636.

Freely available at:
  https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v8/kalisch07a.html
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0510436

Key contributions:
- High-dimensional consistency of the PC algorithm
- Shows PC is consistent when d grows with n (e.g., d = O(n^a) for any a < ∞)
- Requires: faithfulness, causal sufficiency, bounded neighborhood size q
- Partial correlation / Fisher-z tests for Gaussian data
- Connection between conditional independence and partial correlations in Gaussians

The pcalg R package implements both PC and GES:
Kalisch et al. (2012), Journal of Statistical Software, 47(11).
