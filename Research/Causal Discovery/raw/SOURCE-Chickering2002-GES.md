# Source: Chickering (2002) — GES

**Full citation:**
David Maxwell Chickering (2002). "Optimal Structure Identification With Greedy Search."
*Journal of Machine Learning Research*, 3(Nov):507–554.

**Free PDF (network policy blocked download):**
https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume3/chickering02b/chickering02b.pdf

**Abstract:**
Proves the Meek Conjecture: if H is an independence map of G, there exists a finite
sequence of edge additions and covered edge reversals in G such that H remains an
independence map after each step, and after all modifications G = H. Consequence:
there exists a two-phase greedy search (GES) that is consistent (converges to true CPDAG
in large samples) if a perfect map exists.

**Key contributions:**
1. Proof of the Meek Conjecture (Theorem 15)
2. Insert and Delete operators on CPDAGs with efficient local scoring
3. GES algorithm (two phases: forward Insert search, backward Delete search)
4. Consistency proof of GES under BDeu/BIC score and faithfulness
