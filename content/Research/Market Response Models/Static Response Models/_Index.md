## Notes

- [[Functional Forms in Marketing]] — CONTAINS: linear, semilog, power/log-log (constant elasticity), multiplicative, exponential (increasing returns), log-reciprocal (S-shaped saturation), Gompertz, modified exponential, logistic, and ADBUDG (Little 1970) forms, plus the SCAN*PRO scanner model, random-coefficients model, and switching models (Goldfeld-Quandt, Maddala-Nelson).
- [[Market Share Models]] — CONTAINS: attraction-based share framework, MCI (multiplicative competitive interaction) model, log-centering transformation, own/cross-share elasticities, MNL (multinomial logit), IIA property, differential-effects/heterogeneous MCI, SUR log-centering estimation, primary-vs-selective demand decomposition (Schultz-Wittink), and nested/random-coefficients (BLP)/probit relaxations.
- [[Aggregation of Relations]] — CONTAINS: aggregation bias, exact aggregation (linear case) theorem, approximate aggregation with second-order correction, random-coefficients model, temporal vs cross-sectional aggregation, and Clarke (1976) temporal-aggregation correction.
- [[Design of Static Response Models]] — CONTAINS: model completeness, variable-selection principles, competitive specification (absolute levels, share of voice, market-share approaches), interaction terms and moderation, feature-multiplier dummy variables, share of voice (SOV), specification checklist, SUR, and ADL.
