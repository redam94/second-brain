# Pre-registration

The practice of defining the entire data-collection and data-analysis protocol ahead of time, before observing the data, to eliminate [[Researcher Degrees of Freedom]].

## Role in Addressing the Garden of Forking Paths

[[Gelman and Loken 2013 - The Garden of Forking Paths]] discuss pre-registration as one solution to the [[Multiple Comparisons Problem]] arising from data-contingent analyses. By pre-specifying analyses, researchers move from Procedure 3 (data-contingent) to Procedure 2 (pre-registered) in their [[Researcher Degrees of Freedom - Theoretical Framework|theoretical framework]].

## Limitations

Gelman and Loken note several limitations:
- For many applied research projects, pre-registration is impractical because the most important hypotheses emerge from looking at data
- In observational fields (political science, economics, sociology), replication with new data is often impossible
- Pre-registration can constrain the most valuable aspects of scientific inquiry

## Alternative: Pre-publication Replication

The authors suggest [[Pre-publication Replication]] as a complementary approach: conducting an exploratory study first, then following it with a confirmatory replication using a pre-registered protocol.
