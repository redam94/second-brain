# Exploratory vs Confirmatory Research

The distinction between research that generates hypotheses from data (exploratory) and research that tests pre-specified hypotheses (confirmatory).

## Relevance to the Garden of Forking Paths

[[Gelman and Loken 2013 - The Garden of Forking Paths]] argue that many studies presented as confirmatory are actually exploratory, because the analysis details are contingent on the observed data. The paper recommends that researchers:

1. View studies with data-contingent analyses as exploratory, regardless of whether p-values are statistically significant
2. Maintain a sharper distinction between exploratory and confirmatory work (following de Groot, 1956 and Tukey, 1977/1980)
3. Pair exploratory studies with confirmatory replications ([[Pre-publication Replication]])

## Historical Roots

- de Groot (1956) -- distinguished "trying and selecting" from confirmatory testing
- Tukey (1977) -- *Exploratory Data Analysis*
- Tukey (1980) -- argued for the need for both exploratory and confirmatory approaches
