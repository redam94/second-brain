# Bayesian Data Analysis, Third Edition

> **Authors**: [[Andrew Gelman]], [[John B. Carlin]], [[Hal S. Stern]], [[David B. Dunson]], [[Aki Vehtari]], [[Donald B. Rubin]]
> **Publisher**: Chapman & Hall/CRC
> **Year**: 2013 (errors fixed as of February 2025)

## Overview

This textbook serves three audiences: an introductory text on Bayesian inference from first principles, a graduate text on effective current approaches to Bayesian modeling and computation, and a handbook of Bayesian methods in applied statistics. The mathematics required includes basic probability and statistics, elementary calculus, and linear algebra.

## Key Themes

- The three steps of Bayesian data analysis: model setup, posterior computation, model checking
- [[Bayes' Theorem]] as the unifying framework
- Emphasis on practice over philosophy
- [[Hierarchical Models]] as a key practical advantage
- Computational methods ([[MCMC]], [[Hamiltonian Monte Carlo]], [[Stan]])
- [[Model Checking]] and [[Posterior Predictive Checking]]
